Word Tilt is a party game for 3 to 12 people who share one iPhone: Charades, played with the phone held at the forehead, and Imposter, a round of secret cards and bluffing. It is built for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. The answers below cover what people ask most. If yours is not among them, write to seo@promastertradingdoo.com.

Writing to support

  • Name your iPhone model, the iOS version, and the version of Word Tilt shown under About in Settings.
  • Say what you did and what you expected to see. A screenshot helps but is not needed.
  • For a problem with a word, add the deck, the words language and the word itself.
  • No account, order number or personal details are needed.

The tilt does not react, or reacts the wrong way

  • Open Settings (the gear above the decks), then Tilt Check. It shows the angle live and lets you try the flash, the buzz and the tone.
  • Set Tilt sensitivity to High if a small tilt should count, or to Low if answers fire too easily.
  • If your group holds the phone the other way round, switch on Swap tilt directions.
  • Tilting is never required. During a turn, the right half of the screen means Got it and the left half means Pass.

The same word came back

  • A word does not return until every word of the selected decks has been shown. The history is kept between launches, for each deck and each words language.
  • "Start this deck over", on a deck's sheet, empties the history of that deck. "Reset all word history" in Settings empties all of it.

Decks of your own

  • Tap New deck on the shelf, name it, pick its words language, and add words one at a time or with Paste a list.
  • A deck can be picked from 8 words and is used in Imposter from 10. One deck holds up to 500 words.
  • "Delete deck", in the editor of a deck, removes it. Deleting Word Tilt from the iPhone removes every deck.

Language

  • The menus follow the language of the iPhone: English, German or Spanish.
  • The words language is a separate choice, made on first launch and changed later in Settings. A group can play with German words while the menus are in English.

Imposter cards

  • Press and hold the button to see your card, let go to hide it, then pass the phone on.
  • In Settings, Imposter card can be switched from Hold to Tap. With VoiceOver on, tapping is always used.

Sound and haptics

  • Sound and Haptics are switches in Settings. The tones are made inside the app, and the silent switch of the iPhone mutes them.

Your data

  • Word Tilt has no account and no network code. Decks, player names, word history and settings stay on your iPhone, and deleting the app erases them. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
