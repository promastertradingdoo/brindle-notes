Word Tilt is a party game for a group that shares one iPhone. It offers charades, played with the phone held up at the guesser's forehead, and Imposter, a round of secret cards and bluffing. On this page, "the provider" means PROMASTER TRADING DOO, who publishes Word Tilt on the App Store and answers for this policy.

The essentials

  • Word Tilt has no account to create and was built without any networking code. During play it gets in touch with nobody, the provider included.
  • The app does not measure how it is used and does not follow anyone across other apps or websites.
  • Decks, player names, word history and settings are saved on the iPhone that runs the game, and nowhere else.
  • The provider learns something personal about you only if you send the provider an email.

Stored on the device

These items sit in Word Tilt's sandbox, a storage space iOS seals off from all other apps.

  • Word history. For each deck and each words language: the shuffled order of its words and how far the group has come. This is what stops a word from turning up again before the rest of the deck has been played.
  • Decks you make. Each deck's name, its language and the words you typed or pasted in.
  • Player names. Whatever was typed on a setup sheet, so the next game can begin with the same group. A nickname works just as well, and a field may also stay blank.
  • Settings and choices. The words language, which decks are selected, sound, haptics, the tilt directions, tilt sensitivity, appearance, turn and discussion timers and similar options.
  • Error notes. If something fails, such as saving a file, starting the sound or turning the screen, a brief troubleshooting message goes to the iOS log on the iPhone. Player names and deck words are never part of it.

How to clear it. "Start this deck over", on a deck's detail sheet, empties that deck's word history; "Reset all word history" in Settings empties all of it. A deck you made goes away with "Delete deck" in its editor. Names can be erased or typed over on the setup sheet. Deleting Word Tilt from the iPhone wipes every item in this section.

Backups. Copies of the iPhone that you make to iCloud or to your Mac or PC can contain these files too. Such copies are yours to manage in iCloud or on the computer, and the provider cannot get into them.

Motion sensor, sound and other features of the iPhone

During a charades turn and on the Tilt Check screen, Word Tilt reads the phone's motion sensor to tell a downward tilt from an upward one. Each reading is used the instant it arrives and then dropped: none is saved, and none leaves the device. iOS does not require a permission for this kind of reading, and Word Tilt does not ask for any permission.

The tones you hear are produced inside the app. Word Tilt never turns on the microphone and has no access to the camera, photos, contacts, location or health data. Notifications are never sent. Word Tilt tracks no one, so Apple's App Tracking Transparency dialog never comes into play.

Privacy Policy and Support in Settings simply open the matching web page in Safari or your default browser.

When you email the provider

Word Tilt cannot send email on its own. An email to seo@promastertradingdoo.com hands the provider its From line (address and display name), the subject, the body and any files you add. It serves to answer you and, when it reports a problem such as a strange word in a deck, to put the problem right. Nobody else gets it, apart from the mail service that runs the provider's inbox, and it is never sold or put to promotional use. When the conversation has ended, the provider deletes it; you may ask for deletion sooner.

Where the GDPR, or the Swiss or British law modelled on it, applies, the provider handles your email on the basis of legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f), replying being the natural purpose of a message written to a contact address.

Reports that come from Apple

App Store Connect shows the provider aggregate figures, for example the number of installs by storefront. If you switched on "Share With App Developers" (in the iPhone's privacy settings, under Analytics & Improvements), the provider may in addition receive crash logs and usage statistics through Apple. That sharing is Apple's own programme and follows Apple's privacy terms; the reports leave out your name, email address and phone number, and the provider reads them only to find and fix faults. Your choice there can be reversed at any moment.

Younger players

Word Tilt is meant for mixed groups, and its built-in decks carry age-group labels to help pick suitable words. The game never asks how old anyone is and collects personal information from no one, children included. A name typed in for a player stays on the iPhone. Parents or guardians who learn that their child wrote to the provider can have that correspondence removed by asking.

Your rights over personal data

Privacy laws in many places, among them the GDPR with its Swiss and British equivalents, California's CCPA and Mexico's federal law on personal data held by private parties, entitle you to steer what happens to data about you: you can ask what is kept and receive a copy, have mistakes fixed, have the data removed, object to its use or restrict it, and bring a complaint before the authority that supervises data protection where you live. Here that can only concern emails you have sent, because the app itself gives the provider nothing. Write to seo@promastertradingdoo.com; replies come within the time limit of the law that protects you. The provider does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
